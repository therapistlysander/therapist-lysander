How therapy works

The therapy process

Therapy begins with a free, no-commitment introduction call. After our call, you'll complete a brief pre-intake questionnaire to help me prepare. Then we schedule your intake session — there is no pressure to continue at any step.

Free

Free Introduction Call

We begin with a free online introduction call to briefly explore your current situation, your goals for therapy, and whether we feel like a good fit to work together.

This conversation also offers space for practical questions about the therapy process. No commitment required.

2
Online

Pre-Intake Questionnaire

A short, confidential questionnaire to help me understand your needs before our first session.

3

Intake Session

If we decide to continue, we start with a more in-depth intake session. During this session, we explore your background, current difficulties, relevant life experiences, and treatment goals in greater detail.

Following the intake, a treatment plan is developed outlining the main complaints, therapeutic goals, and proposed treatment approach.

4
60 minutes · €110

Ongoing Sessions

Follow-up sessions are tailored to your individual needs and therapeutic goals. Depending on the situation, therapy may focus on:

  • Trauma processing
  • Emotional regulation
  • Anxiety and avoidance patterns
  • Self-worth and relational dynamics
  • Behavioral change
  • Nervous system regulation
  • Developing healthier coping patterns
Practical details

Session information

Online sessions

Sessions take place in a secure, confidential online setting. Available to clients worldwide. Research shows online therapy is as effective as in-person for most psychological difficulties.

Session duration

Sessions are 60 minutes. The free introduction call is 30 minutes. Sessions are typically weekly in the early phase of therapy, adapting to your needs and progress over time.

Languages

Sessions are conducted in Dutch or English. Both languages are equally available for all therapy modalities, including EMDR, Schema Therapy, and other approaches.
