Therapeutic methods

Evidence-based approaches
tailored to you

I work integratively, drawing from multiple proven methods to address the root causes of your difficulties — not just the symptoms.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

CBT helps you identify and change unhelpful thought patterns and behaviours that maintain distress. We work together to recognise negative automatic thoughts, challenge their validity, and develop healthier cognitive patterns — resulting in lasting improvements in mood, anxiety, and self-esteem.

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

ACT shifts the focus from fighting difficult thoughts and feelings to developing psychological flexibility. By identifying your core values and committing to actions aligned with them, you can build a meaningful life even in the presence of inner pain — reducing the grip that anxiety, depression, or self-criticism holds over you.

Eye Movement Desensitisation & Reprocessing (EMDR)

EMDR is one of the most evidence-based treatments for trauma and PTSD. Using bilateral stimulation, EMDR helps the brain process and integrate traumatic memories that have become "stuck." EMDR can also be highly effective in treating intense fears, catastrophic future scenarios, and intrusive "flashforward" images that keep people stuck in anxiety and avoidance.

Schema Therapy & Parts Work

Schema therapy addresses deep-rooted emotional patterns — "schemas" — formed in childhood that drive recurring difficulties in adult life. Combined with parts work, we bring care and understanding to wounded inner parts, replacing maladaptive coping with genuine emotional healing and self-compassion.

Somatic Psychotherapy

Trauma is held not just in the mind but in the body. Somatic approaches address how stress, trauma, and emotion are stored in physical tension, movement patterns, and nervous system responses. Where helpful, I integrate body-based methods — including boxing as a psychotherapeutic intervention — to help you access and release what talk therapy alone cannot reach.

How it works

Starting therapy — what to expect

Free Introduction Call

A free 30-minute online introduction call to briefly explore your current situation, your goals for therapy, and whether we feel like a good fit to work together.

Intake Session

An in-depth 60-minute intake session exploring your background, current difficulties, relevant life experiences, and treatment goals in greater detail.

Treatment Plan

Following the intake, a treatment plan is developed outlining the main complaints, therapeutic goals, and proposed treatment approach — tailored to you.

Ongoing Sessions

Follow-up sessions of 60 minutes, tailored to your individual needs. Sessions are active, collaborative, and adapted to your pace and needs.
