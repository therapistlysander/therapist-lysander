About Lysander

Psychologist & Trauma Specialist

A compassionate, pragmatic approach — drawing from lived experience, evidence-based methods, and genuine care for every person I work with.

Who I am

Lysander Verschuur, MSc.

I am a trained psychologist working with individuals experiencing psychological and emotional difficulties such as trauma, anxiety, depression, and self-esteem issues. I am here to support people through some of life's hardest chapters.

My work is focused on the treatment and reduction of mental health complaints of individual clients, using evidence-based therapeutic methods. I work with both Dutch-speaking and English-speaking clients.

I help clients move from states of overwhelm, constriction, and emotional distress toward greater stability, clarity, and psychological flexibility.

Personal journey

A therapist who has been there

For years, I struggled with trauma, anxiety, and a harsh inner critic. I know firsthand how deeply these patterns can affect your life — the way they shape your relationships, your sense of self, and your ability to feel at home in the world. This personal experience informs my work — not as a replacement for clinical methods, but as something that deepens empathy, understanding, and precision in therapy.

Everything I offer in therapy — from EMDR and ACT to mindfulness and values-based living — I've lived myself. I've practised it, wrestled with it, and integrated it. This personal experience shapes how I work: compassionate, pragmatic, non-judgmental, and fully committed to your growth.

Therapy with me isn't just about symptom relief — it's about finding your way back to yourself, grounded in self-understanding, self-worth, and personal empowerment.

Core perspective

A trauma-focused lens

At the heart of much psychological distress lies trauma — sometimes obvious, sometimes hidden beneath layers of beliefs, behaviours, or symptoms. I view many mental health struggles as rooted in difficult, painful, or invalidating past experiences.

I apply a trauma-focused lens throughout my work. Where helpful, I integrate specific trauma treatments to neutralise the emotional charge of painful memories and help you develop a more grounded and compassionate relationship with yourself.

"Sometimes the bravest thing is to stay — to look at what has been difficult and find out what it truly needs."

Trauma-specific interventions

  • EMDRNeutralise and process deep trauma using bilateral stimulation.
  • Exposure TherapyFace and integrate fear and trauma through graded exposure.
  • Flash TechniqueA gentler entry when trauma floods with dissociation.
  • Imagery RescriptingRewriting painful emotional memories to reduce their distress.
  • Schema Therapy & Parts WorkBringing care to wounded inner parts and survival strategies.
My approach

Tailored, collaborative, direct

Every person carries a unique life story, emotional landscape, and psychological makeup. I tailor each therapy trajectory to fit the person in front of me — identifying and addressing the core of their challenges as directly as possible.

My approach is practical, goal-oriented, and collaborative: we work together to develop insight, emotional resilience, and concrete tools for change.
